Analysis of the relationship between urbanization and economic growth using World Bank data.

# Growth and the City

Urban Economics project studying the relationship between **urbanization and economic growth across countries**.

## Research question

Do countries that experience greater urbanization also experience greater economic growth?

## Data

The analysis uses **World Bank World Development Indicators**:

- Urban population (% of total population)
- GDP per capita

Two approaches were used:

- Cross-sectional analysis for 2023
- Long-term analysis covering 1990–2023

## Methodology

The project compares:

- GDP per capita and urbanization levels
- Changes in urbanization and long-term economic growth
- Cross-country differences
- Correlation between urbanization and economic performance

## Main findings

The analysis shows that richer countries tend to be more urbanized.

The correlation between urbanization and log GDP per capita was approximately **0.73**.

However, the relationship between the pace of urbanization and economic growth was much weaker, with a correlation of approximately **0.15**.

This suggests that countries that urbanize faster do not necessarily grow faster.
